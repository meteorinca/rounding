Round Down (0-4)
If the digit to the right is 0, 1, 2, 3, or 4, keep the target digit the same.
73 → 70 (ones digit is 3, less than 5)
Round Up (5-9)
If the digit to the right is 5, 6, 7, 8, or 9, increase the target digit by 1.
68 → 70 (ones digit is 8, 5 or more)
📚 Rounding Tips & Methods
1 Find the Target Digit
Decide which place you're rounding to: tens, hundreds, or thousands. Circle or underline that digit.
2 Look to the Right
Check the digit immediately to the RIGHT of your target digit. This is the "decider" digit!
3 5 or More? Round Up!
If the "decider" digit is 5, 6, 7, 8, or 9 → add 1 to your target digit and turn everything after to zeros. If it's 0, 1, 2, 3, 4 → keep the target digit and turn everything after to zeros.
💡 Multi-Step Problems
For word problems: Solve all the math steps FIRST to get an exact answer, THEN round that final answer to the place the problem asks for.
