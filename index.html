Rounding & Estimation

Rounding means making a number simpler but still close to the original.

Look at the digit to the right of the place you’re rounding to. 5 or more → round up. 4 or less → stay.

Tip: Write your rounded answer in the box for each problem.

Quick Steps

  1. Find the place you are rounding to.
  2. Look one digit to the right.
  3. 5–9: round up. 0–4: stay the same.
